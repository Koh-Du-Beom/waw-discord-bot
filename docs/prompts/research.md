# Research

Read `AGENTS.md`, relevant policies, current research, and ADRs.

Research the requested decision without implementing production code.

1. State the decision question.
2. Link relevant requirement IDs.
3. Derive evaluation criteria.
4. Compare realistic alternatives, including the strongest alternative to the provisional recommendation.
5. Prefer official documentation and primary sources.
6. Separate verified facts, inference, assumptions, and unknowns.
7. Assess security, data, operations, cost, maintainability, migration, rollback, and vendor lock-in.
8. Identify evidence gaps requiring a Spike.
9. Save the result under `docs/research/technology-options/`.
10. Do not mark an ADR Accepted.

End with a provisional recommendation, strongest alternative, risks, required validation, reversal conditions, and the exact next prompt.
