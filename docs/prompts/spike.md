# Spike

Read `AGENTS.md` and the related research.

Run a minimal, reversible experiment for the stated hypothesis.

1. Define one hypothesis.
2. Define success and failure criteria.
3. Keep scope small and avoid production architecture.
4. Record environment and exact commands.
5. Collect evidence.
6. Document limitations and uncertainty.
7. Store the report under `docs/research/spikes/`.
8. Do not silently turn the Spike into product code.
9. Remove temporary secrets and generated artifacts that should not be committed.

Report the conclusion, evidence, reproduction steps, cleanup, and impact on the pending decision.
